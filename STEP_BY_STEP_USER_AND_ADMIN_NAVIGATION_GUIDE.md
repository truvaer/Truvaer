# TruvAer Aviation Intelligence & Operations Platform
## Step-by-Step Navigation & Operational Systems Guide
*Comprehensive end-to-end operational manual for Standard Aviators, Verified Business Enterprises, and Administrative Personnel.*

---

## 1. System Ecosystem & Entry Points

TruvAer is structured into two dedicated operational environments designed for complete operational separation and strict security isolation:

| Portal Interface | Target Audience | Primary Functionality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`truvaer.com`** | Public Aviators, Flight Crew, Trainees, and Verified Aviation Businesses | Directory discovery, quotation inquiries, social marketing feed, B2C booking, corporate wallet, and messaging. |
| **`admin.truvaer.com`** | Operations Directorate, HR, Finance Treasury, Compliance, and Super Admins | Admin PMS, C-Panel governance, regional pricing matrix, Credit Management System (CMS), IAM clearance, and audit trails. |

*Note: Both interfaces share a unified, hardened authentication design: Enter Email & Password $\rightarrow$ Enter 2-Step Security MPIN $\rightarrow$ Direct routing to the designated Home Dashboard (preventing accidental redirection to secondary modules).*

## 2. Standard Aviator User Journey (`truvaer.com`)

### 2.1 Sign-In & Authentication
1. Navigate to `truvaer.com`.
2. Click **Login** in the top navigation bar.
3. Input your registered aviation email address and password.
4. When prompted, enter your **4-Digit Security MPIN** for 2-step authentication.
5. The system validates session integrity and automatically directs you to the **Home Page**.

### 2.2 Global Aviation Directory & Service Discovery
1. Click **Directory** from the primary navigation bar.
2. Filter providers by category:
   - *Private Air Charter & Executive Jet Operations*
   - *MRO & Turbine Overhaul Centers*
   - *Commercial Pilot Training & Cadet Academies*
   - *Air Cargo & Heavy Lift Logistics*
   - *Avionics & Spare Parts Suppliers*
3. Use the location dropdown to filter by Country (e.g., India, United States, UAE, United Kingdom, Singapore).
4. Click on any provider card to inspect their **Verified Profile**, certification credentials, fleet composition, and physical coordinates.

### 2.3 Submitting Quotations & Direct Aviator Communications
1. From any business page or search card, click **Request Quote / Inquire**.
2. Select inquiry type (Flight Charter, Maintenance Shop Visit, Cadet Admission, Avionics Part).
3. Specify route/details (Departure/Arrival airports, date range, passengers/cargo payload).
4. Click **Send Request**. The request is logged in your **Inquiries** tab and delivered instantly to the enterprise dispatch desk.
5. Access the **Chat** module to engage in real-time encrypted messaging directly with verified business representatives.

### 2.4 Digital Aviator Wallet & Balance Management
1. Click the **Wallet** icon in the header.
2. View your real-time balance of **Purchased Credits** and **Earned Promotional Credits**.
3. To top up: Select a credit bundle (e.g., 500 Cr, 2,500 Cr, 10,000 Cr), choose Razorpay or Corporate Wire, and complete payment.
4. Credits are minted instantly to your account with cryptographic HMAC verification.

## 3. Verified Aviation Business Journey (`truvaer.com`)

### 3.1 Business Profile & Entity Verification
1. Access **Business Dashboard** via the profile avatar menu.
2. Ensure enterprise registration details are complete:
   - PAN / TAN / Corporate Registration Number
   - Nature of Business & Aviation Taxonomy Category
   - Physical Hangar / Office Location
   - Settlement Bank Coordinates (IFSC / SWIFT / Masked Account Number)
3. Account passes through the **Compliance Gate** to receive the green **✓ VERIFIED** badge.

### 3.2 Creating Official Publications (Hybrid Media Upload)
1. Navigate to the **Feed / Publications** section.
2. Click **Create Official Publication**.
3. **Title & Body**: Enter article headline and detailed operational update (e.g., fleet induction, shop visit capacity, cadet openings).
4. **Hybrid Media Attachment Section**:
   - **Drag & Drop**: Drag any JPG, PNG, or MP4 file directly into the dashed drop zone, or click to browse your local device (supports files up to 50MB).
   - **Alternative Link**: Click **"Or paste media URL"** to supply a direct HTTPS image or video link.
   - **Preview Container**: Once selected, the drop zone automatically collapses to show an image or video thumbnail with a **Remove ("X")** button to clear or replace.
5. **Sponsored Marketing Boost (Condition 4 Compliant)**:
   - Toggle **"Market This Post (Sponsored Campaign Boost)"**.
   - Note: In strict compliance with Condition 4, sponsored boosts are delivered exclusively to **Standard Aviators** and are never served to competitor B2B accounts.
   - **Lifecycle Dependent Trigger**:
     * Select **Standard New Publication**: Location dropdown populates with individual **Countries**.
     * Select **Renew and Extend**: Location dropdown dynamically switches to populated **Aviation Regions / Flight Corridors** (APAC, EMEA, NA, LATAM, MENA, SAIO, GLOBAL).
   - **Multi-Country Selection**: Select multiple target countries; real-time calculation computes the dynamic additive credit total.
6. Click **Publish Official Publication**.

### 3.3 Quantitative Reporting Interface
1. In the publication feed or business desk, click **Quantitative Reporting** on any sponsored campaign.
2. Single-line aggregate overview header displays:
   - Aggregate Overview selector
   - Refresh button
   - Close button
3. Real-time verified metrics:
   - Total Views / Impressions
   - Target Aviator Audience Reached (Condition 4 verified: 100% consumer aviators)
   - Clicks & Direct Engagement Rate
   - Total Credit Usage / Spend (with base rate equivalent)
4. Click **Renew / Extend** on any campaign row to extend validity while preserving historical impressions and analytics.

## 4. Operations Directorate & Administrative PMS (`admin.truvaer.com`)

### 4.1 Admin Sign-In & Access Isolation
1. Access the administrative portal at `admin.truvaer.com`.
2. Input administrative email (mandated organizational domain `@yarifly.com` or `@truvaer.com`).
3. Enter administrative password and your dedicated **4-Digit Security MPIN**.
4. Authentication validates role clearance and lands directly on the **Operations & C-Panel Desk**.

### 4.2 Operations Sector Sub-Modules
The Operations sub-navigation bar is cleanly aligned:
- **ERP Platform Overview**: Live directory of Corporate Business Accounts with additive filters (Audit, Lock Status, Lifecycle State, Debit Status).
- **Corporate Account Actions (Dedicated Pop-Up)**:
  * Click the three-dot button (`•••`) on any corporate business account row.
  * A full, non-clipped modal pop-up displays all 6 operational actions:
    1. *Edit Business Profile* (Update registration, PAN, category, PII)
    2. *Initiate Debit Request* (Deduct credits with administrative justification)
    3. *Bank Coordinates Audit* (Verify or revoke settlement bank remittance status)
    4. *Apply / Lift Hard Lock* (Immediate administrative security suspension or restoration)
    5. *Deactivate / Reactivate Account* (Lifecycle operational state management)
    6. *Delete Corporate Account* (Permanent registry purge for L8 Super Admin)
- **Admin PMS (Project Management System)**:
  * Manage engineering and operations tasks with priority tags (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
  * Assign tasks to specific admin personnel and track completion stages.
- **Services & Verification Desk**: Audit newly submitted aviation services and listings.
- **Regional & Country Mapping**: Map ISO country codes to regional civil aviation authorities (DGCA, FAA, EASA, GCAA).
- **Content Queue**: Audit pending job submissions and editorial news articles before public distribution.

## 5. Sovereign Super Admin & Financial Governance (Levels 7 & 8)

### 5.1 Centralized Pricing Matrix (Super Admin Panel)
1. In `Admin C-Panel`, navigate to **Sector: Finance** $\rightarrow$ **Centralized Pricing Matrix**.
2. **Dashboard View**:
   - Inspect all active pricing rules by Location Name, Type (Country / Region), Credit Cost, and Last Updated.
   - Global Default Baseline Rate card (fallback rate applied to unconfigured locations).
3. **Add / Edit Pricing Rule Modal**:
   - Choose Location Type: **Country** or **Region Flight Corridor**.
   - Select Location from searchable list.
   - Set custom Credit Cost (e.g., US = 15 Cr, UK = 12 Cr, APAC Corridor = 30 Cr).
   - Enter administrative notes and click **Save Pricing Rule**.
4. **Dynamic Real-Time Calculation Engine**:
   - When users select multiple countries, credit costs add together dynamically (e.g., US 15 + UK 12 = 27 Cr/day).
   - Regional overrides lock out individual countries within that flight corridor to prevent double-charging.
   - Any country without custom pricing charges the Global Default rate automatically.

### 5.2 Unified Credit Management System (CMS Desk)
Access via **Admin C-Panel** $\rightarrow$ **Finance Sector** $\rightarrow$ **Credit Management System (CMS)**:

1. **Pillar 1: Central Dashboard**:
   - Real-time global credit exposure, active utilization percentage, liquid headroom reserve, and overdue at-risk balances.
   - Quick Actions: Trigger Global Exposure Threshold Alert, Emergency Account Freeze toggle, and CSV audit export.
   - Departmental credit exposure breakdown (Executive Charters, MRO, Pilot Academies, Air Cargo).

2. **Pillar 2: Credit Allocation (Easy Editing Engine)**:
   - Searchable corporate account directory.
   - Click **Adjust** on any client:
     * Instant baseline limit input with quick adjustment buttons (+5k, +10k, -5k Cr).
     * Toggle **Grant Temporary Credit Extension** (+amount, validity for 7, 14, or 30 days).
     * Mandatory audit rationale is strictly enforced.
     * Limit increases $>5,000$ Cr by sub-administrators automatically route to the **Pending Approval Queue** for Level 8 Super Admin sign-off.

3. **Pillar 3: Risk Assessment**:
   - Internal Payment History Score (0 - 100 score tracking on-time remittances).
   - External Bureau Rating Tier: Dun & Bradstreet / Equifax simulated commercial grades (AAA, AA, A, BBB, BB, B, Default Warning).
   - Click **Re-Evaluate** on any client to run the dynamic risk scoring algorithm.

4. **Pillar 4: A/R & Ledger**:
   - Real-time balance tracking synchronized with billing.
   - Click **Record Manual Adjustment**:
     * Issue **Credit Memo** (billing concession or service credit).
     * Apply **Promotional Discount**.
     * Execute **Bad Debt Write-Off** (strictly restricted to L7/L8 clearance).
   - Synchronized Ledger Journal displays reference IDs, amounts, and running balances.

5. **Pillar 5: Collections & Aging Workflows**:
   - Receivables sorted into 4 aging buckets: Current (0-30 days), Mild (31-60 days), Severe (61-90 days), Critical (90+ days).
   - Multi-stage Dunning Escalation:
     * Stage 1: Friendly Courtesy Reminder
     * Stage 2: Urgent Demand & Pre-Freeze Warning
     * Stage 3: Sovereign Legal Demand & Asset Hard Lock
   - Click **Log Promise** to register client promise-to-pay date, committed amount, and collector notes.

6. **Pillar 6: Admin & Tamper-Proof Audit Trail**:
   - Level 7 & 8 clearance badge verification.
   - Immutable log table detailing Audit ID, UTC Timestamp, Entity Name, Action Type, Previous Value $\rightarrow$ New Value, Authorized Actor, and Mandatory Rationale.
   - Click **Download CSV Audit Report** to export compliance records for external institutional auditing.

### 5.3 FlashNews Live Blink Feed Desk (UPPERCASE Enforced)
1. Navigate to **Sector: Publication** $\rightarrow$ **FlashNews & Blink Feed Desk**.
2. **Uppercase Enforcement**: In strict compliance with platform standards, all headlines and topics are automatically transformed and stored in **UPPERCASE ONLY**.
3. Set Display Duration, Priority Level, Target Country, and click **Publish Live FlashNews**.
4. The 5-second ticker renders on aviator dashboards with pause/resume toggle controls that pause rotation without causing headlines to vanish.

### 5.4 Human Resources & Admin Onboarding
1. Navigate to **Sector: HR** $\rightarrow$ **Role Switch & IAM Governance**.
2. Click **Onboard Admin Personnel**.
3. Provide full name, organizational email (`@yarifly.com` or `@truvaer.com`), assigned Department, and Authorization Level (L1 to L8).
4. System dispatches a formal **Welcome Email Notification** containing:
   - Admin Portal URL (`admin.truvaer.com`)
   - Username (Email Address)
   - Secure Password Setup Link (`admin.truvaer.com/set-password?token=...`)
5. Authorized L7 & L8 Super Admins can click **Reset Password Link** on any personnel row to regenerate access links at any time.

## 6. Continuous Integration & Production Deployment (CI/CD)

The TruvAer production pipeline adheres to modern enterprise GitOps architecture:

```
[Developer Workspace]
       │
       ▼ Git Commit & Tag (vX.Y.Z)
 [GitHub Repository] ──► Pull Request Security Check
       │
       ▼ Trigger (GitHub Actions / Webhook)
 [Continuous Integration (CI)]
   ├── 1. Build Verification (Vite / TypeScript check)
   ├── 2. Automated Test Suite (Linters, Security audits)
   └── 3. Docker Container Packaging
       │
       ▼ Push
 [Artifact Registry] (Docker Hub / AWS ECR / Azure ACR)
       │
       ▼ Automated Rollout (Blue-Green / Rolling Deployment)
 [Production Cluster] (EC2 / Azure App Service / Kubernetes)
   ├── Zero-Downtime Traffic Switch (Users experience no session disruption)
   ├── Post-Deployment Smoke Tests
   └── Live Telemetry & Health Monitoring
```

*This automated CI/CD pipeline ensures that when new versions are tagged and pushed, updates roll out seamlessly to production without disturbing active aviator sessions or risking data loss.*
